## NAIC 2026 / N-ATLAS

[Official programme and linked eligibility/submission anchors](https://ncair.nitda.gov.ng/naic/) describe 2026 applications closing **12 October, 11:59 PM WAT**. Voice-First Access requires official N-ATLAS ASR for the relevant language and **50 documented real interactions**. General conditions include Nigerian citizenship/entities, original work not previously awarded, one problem statement and English materials. Enterprise applicants need adult individual ID or CAC evidence; diaspora teams need half their members in Nigeria. Academia additionally needs student/research participation, supervision and institutional endorsement.

Working artefact, integration proof, actual validation, technical documentation, 3–5-minute demo, team details and track documents are required. Team-size wording conflicts: track cards say 2–5; the enterprise FAQ says 1–6. Resolve before applying.

The linked [enterprise application](https://ondi.innox.africa/apply/naic/naic-innovation-enterprise) exposed no readable rules to the retrieval tool. No separate linked rulebook was found. Official ASR service access, local-weight acceptance and dual-entry/reuse treatment remain unresolved. **Integration and real-user gates are unmet.**

## Zenith Zecathon

[Official Beyond Limits page](https://beyondlimits.global/zecathonhackathon/) identifies **6.0**, with 2026 applications **22 September–13 October**, Lagos build **20–22 October**, semifinal **23 October**, final **28 October**. Deadline time/timezone is unstated. It also retains 2025 dates, inconsistent prize blocks and two track lists. **Smart SME Financing and Growth Platform** appears in the newer list; its applicability should be confirmed.

Published criteria: African citizens aged 20–35, teams of 2–4, relevant conflict exclusions, original team-owned work and travel to Lagos if selected. The FAQ permits concept-stage applications; requested materials include problem, solution/technology, impact, implementation, team and pitch deck. This product's financing-evidence positioning is an inference, not acceptance or compliance.

The linked Google application form could not be retrieved; any additional form terms remain unverified. Reuse, prior awards, dual entry, current tracks and exact deadline cutoff need confirmation. No application or organiser message has been sent.
