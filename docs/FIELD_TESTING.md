# Consented trader validation kit

**Real sessions currently recorded: 0.** Automated fixtures, browser tests and developer demonstrations are not real trader validation. Leave metrics blank until observed. Do not count app actions as participants or silently count repeated system retries as interactions.

## Facilitator script

“We are testing a prototype for recording business activity. It does not provide a loan or score. Participation is optional. Please use made-up customer aliases and no bank details. We will record anonymous task outcomes and your feedback. Audio processing needs separate permission; recordings are discarded after the request. You can stop at any time or ask us to remove your evaluation information. Live speech is currently [state the actual verified capability; if blocked, say blocked]. Do you consent?”

Record explicit consent before opening the evaluation form. Assign a random ID such as P-A7K2, keeping any necessary withdrawal mapping separately and privately. Never store a participant name in the app. Do not describe manual/fixture tests as real N-ATLAS interactions.

## Tasks

1. Ambiguous sale: five bags of rice, forty thousand. Observe whether the trader understands the total/per-bag and payment questions. Do not coach unless they ask; record assistance.
2. Credit sale of ₦40,000 and later ₦15,000 collection. Ask what is still owed.
3. Correct an unconfirmed draft, then a confirmed entry without later payment links. Ask what was changed and what history remains.
4. Query today's sales and review the difference between sales and cash received.
5. Import and corroborate a synthetic transfer with a facilitator. Ask whether anything was counted twice.
6. Review the private report and explain who can access it and what revocation means.

In Help & privacy, record participant ID, language, task, expected/confirmed amount, result, correction, elapsed seconds, outcome and brief feedback. The export includes consent time and observation timestamp. Completion outcome is completed without help / assisted / failed. Do not use demo businesses for real evaluation.

## Measurement

Report actual session count and unique participant count separately. Exact amount accuracy denominator includes only sessions with both expected and confirmed amounts. Completion denominator is all observed sessions, with assisted completion separately reported. Report median/mean duration with sample size; app shows mean only. Record transcription quality and numeric extraction separately, by language, device and noise context, once the actual provider works. Do not generalise a convenience sample to all traders.
