# Product demonstration script (3–5 minutes)

Use a synthetic merchant and synthetic values. Do not show a real participant's records, credentials, private share token, or database configuration. Before recording, verify the live service with a consented test. If it is unavailable, say so and demonstrate manual entry or the visibly labelled fixture; do not call a fixture live speech recognition.

1. **0:00–0:30 — Problem.** Introduce MarketPulse Voice for small Nigerian businesses that need a clear record of sales, expenses, collections and supplier payments.
2. **0:30–1:10 — Private workspace.** Sign in with a prepared synthetic account and show an empty or synthetic business book. Explain that records are scoped to the account.
3. **1:10–2:05 — Voice path.** Show language selection and explicit audio consent. If N-ATLAS is live, record one short synthetic transaction and show the transcript and suggestion. State that the model can be wrong and the record is not saved yet. If live ASR is unavailable, use manual entry and clearly identify it as manual.
4. **2:05–2:45 — Review and save.** Correct a field, read the review, explicitly confirm, then show the saved ledger item. Explain that drafts do not count in totals.
5. **2:45–3:30 — Business summary.** Show sales versus cash received and a debt/expense summary. Clarify that cash movement is not accounting profit.
6. **3:30–4:10 — Privacy and fallback.** Show that manual entry works without AI and explain audio retention and gateway configuration honestly.
7. **4:10–4:40 — Evidence.** State the actual live integration test status and actual user-validation count. Do not claim 50 interactions unless the consented records support it.

Trim or expand naturally to fit the 3–5 minute limit. This is a script template, not a recorded video.
